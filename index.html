      What’s a SIP?

      A Systematic Investment Plan: a fixed amount moves from your savings into investments every month, automatically.

      • 💤 Savings earn 2.5% a year while prices rise about 6%.
      • 📈 Invested money grows 10–15% a year over any 10 years, with ups and downs along the way.
      • ⏰ Starting early matters most: time does the heavy lifting.
      • 🛟 Keep about 6 months of expenses in savings for surprises.

      Tip: start a SIP as soon as money is left over each month.
