Life is a race.
Money is the fuel.

Race from 23 to 80. Keep yourself, your people and your community happy, and don’t run out of fuel. Goa trips, weddings, kids, taxes, layoffs and very persuasive sales will try to slow you down.

🏁Reach 80🎯3 life rings🎲Random life events🌱Monthly SIPs🏡Bikes, cars, homes
How it works: every month your pay comes in and your bills go out; what is left builds your savings. Big life moments pause the race so you can choose; small surprises slide onto the track as cards while you keep racing; every 5 years a pit stop lets you retune your pace, lifestyle and SIP. Your score comes from three life rings (🧍 Me, 👨‍👩‍👧 Family & Friends, 🏘️ Community), mostly the weakest one. Six months in a row of unpaid bills ends the race early.
